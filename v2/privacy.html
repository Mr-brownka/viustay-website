This notice explains what personal data Viustay collects through this website, why, and your rights under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

What we collect

  • Home seekers: your name, WhatsApp number, the homes you pick, your viewing day and time, and what you're looking for (area, budget, must-haves).
  • Owners, managers and caretakers: your name, WhatsApp number, email, your role, the building's name and location, and your vacant units and rents.
  • Dashboard users: your email and a password (stored securely by our database provider; we can't see your password).

Why we use it

  • To arrange viewings, match you with homes and contact you about them on WhatsApp or phone.
  • To list and market vacant units, and to show managers their units, viewings and placement fees.

We don't sell your data. Managers see viewing dates and times for their units, but not tenants' names or phone numbers.

Where it's stored and for how long

Data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, whose servers may be outside Kenya. We keep enquiry details for up to [12 MONTHS] after our last contact, then delete them, unless we need them for an active tenancy or a legal reason.
