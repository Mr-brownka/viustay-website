Building the digital backbone of Africa's rental market.

Viustay is a Nairobi proptech company. We start with placements and build the software that runs renting on top of them.

The problem

Renting in Nairobi runs on phone calls, WhatsApp forwards and unverified listings. Tenants waste days on viewings; landlords lose months of rent to empty units; managers track it all on paper and spreadsheets.

How we grow

  1. 1
    Placements (now)

    Verified listings, guided search and organised viewings. Each placement earns a fee and adds a building, a manager and a tenant to our network.

  2. 2
    Tools for managers (next)

    A dashboard for vacancies, viewings and fees, growing into rent collection, maintenance and tenant screening.

  3. 3
    Tools for tenants and guests (later)

    An app that stays with renters after move-in: payments, requests, renewals and services.

Why us

We visit every home ourselves, so our data is real. Every placement, viewing and enquiry feeds a growing picture of what renters want and what buildings offer. That is the base for smart matching, pricing and, in time, AI tools across the rental lifecycle.
