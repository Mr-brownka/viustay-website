About Viustay

We make renting in Nairobi simple.

We visit every home ourselves, match you with homes that fit, and handle everything to move-in.

Finding a home here usually means endless calls, fake listings, agents who don't show up, and "viewing fees" for houses that were taken last week. Landlords have the opposite problem: empty units, wasted viewings, and tenants who aren't a fit.

Viustay fixes both sides.

We visit and photograph every home ourselves, so what you see is what's there. You tell us what you need, we shortlist homes that fit, and we take you to view them in one trip. Then we handle the landlord and the paperwork, all the way to move-in.

Where we're going

Placements are just the start. We're building the digital backbone for renting in Africa: tools that help managers fill and run their buildings, and an app that stays with tenants and guests long after they move in.

What we stand for

Verified, always.Every listing is visited by us. Real photos, real prices.
Your time matters.One trip, matched homes, no wasted viewings.
Fair to both sides.Clear fees, no surprises, and good tenants for good landlords.
